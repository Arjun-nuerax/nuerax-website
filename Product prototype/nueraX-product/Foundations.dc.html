Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the height of the butterfly’s wing on all sides. Minimum width: 120px digital, 24mm print.

Below 32px, use the single-wing “X” mark for legibility. Ship PNG at 512 / 192 / 180 / 144 / 96 / 32 / 16.

Do

  • Use the full-color mark on light or dark surfaces
  • Preserve the gradient and aspect ratio
  • Give it room to breathe

Don’t

  • Stretch, rotate or recolor the butterfly
  • Add drop shadows or outlines to the wordmark
  • Place on low-contrast or busy backgrounds

Iconography

Modern line icons on a 24px grid — 1.8px stroke, rounded caps & joins. Tint icons with a single brand color; never mix two brand colors in one glyph.

Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text & UI ≥ 3:1. Brand colors are vivid — pair carefully.

Focus ring

Always-visible 3px focus ring in --ring with a surface-colored offset. Never remove :focus-visible.

Guidelines

  • Never encode meaning with color alone — pair with text or icon
  • Use ink text on pink / cyan / green / orange fills
  • Hit targets ≥ 44×44px; label every icon button
