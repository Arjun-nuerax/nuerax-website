How it works

From exam plan to improved rank, in one loop.

Most tools stop at the marks. ekRank keeps going until the student has fixed the mistake.

Improve

FAQs

Questions, answered.

What is ekRank?

ekRank is a digital assessment and student performance platform for IIT-JEE and NEET. It gives leadership, teachers and students one connected place to plan exams, conduct them, understand the results and act on them.

Who sets up our institution?

We do. You share your branches, sections, teachers and students in a simple sheet, and our team loads and checks it with you.

Can we keep running exams on paper?

Yes. Print the paper and answer sheets from ekRank, conduct the exam as usual, then scan and upload the sheets. Results appear alongside your online exams.

Where do the questions come from?

From the ekRank Question Bank of more than 43,000 expert-curated JEE and NEET questions, and from questions your own teachers add.

Are online exams proctored?

Yes. Before starting, each student passes a camera and face check. The exam runs in a locked full-screen window with the webcam on. If a student leaves the screen, moves out of the camera or a second face appears, they are warned and the teacher is alerted live. You choose how many violations are allowed before the exam submits itself, and webcam snapshots are saved so you can review every flag afterwards.

Do the exams follow the real JEE and NEET pattern?

Yes. Papers follow the official JEE Main, JEE Advanced and NEET patterns, with the same sections, question types and marking. For paper exams, students fill an NTA-style OMR sheet that ekRank reads after you scan it.

Which devices are supported?

ekRank runs in the browser on laptops, tablets and phones. No installation is needed to get started.

Can an individual student use ekRank?

Yes. Any JEE or NEET aspirant can start a 30-day free trial of the Student App.
